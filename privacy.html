Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Jev After Dark is a party game made by Pantheon3D. It has no accounts and no analytics, and we do not collect personal data ourselves.

Data saved on your phone

Player names, settings, scores, house cards and your progress are saved only on your device so the app remembers them next time. They are not sent to us and are not included in Android backups. You can remove them in the app or by clearing the app's data.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and information about ad interactions, as described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices in the app's orb menu. Buying the After Dark pass removes ads.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app reports the achievements you unlock to your Play Games profile. Play Games is provided by Google under Google's privacy policy. The app works fully without signing in.

Purchases

Optional purchases (the After Dark pass) are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you turn on game night reminders, the app schedules them on your phone. No data is sent to us, and you can turn them off at any time.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: pantheon3d.contact@gmail.com
